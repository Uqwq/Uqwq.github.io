Need a help?

How do I start using the client?

After purchase, download the loader from your account, launch it and log in. Launch details will be available in your personal account.

What are the system requirements?

Anything that runs Minecraft comfortably will run the client: Windows 10 or 11, 8 GB of RAM and Java 21. A dedicated GPU is not required.

Can I use one key on multiple PCs?

The key is bound to one machine. To move it, reset the HWID in your profile — the service lives there, and support can help if anything goes wrong.
